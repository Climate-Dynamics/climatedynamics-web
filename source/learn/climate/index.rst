Description
------------

**Building a Climate** is a series of lessons that introduces the climate system step-by-step. The lessons start with large-scale controls on the climate system, such as orbital mechanics, and end with small-scale processes like rain formation.

These chapters were developed as part of the `INTEGRATE teaching package <\https://integrate.mutz.science/>`_, which is based on workshops and lectures by Sebastian G. Mutz and supported by the EGU Higher Education Teaching Grant 2020.
